1. Development tools¶

This chapter introduces the development environment used throughout the book and what you need to do in order to complete assignments.

Understanding the first 2 sections of this chapter are critical - most projects are need to be compiled and tested on the Linux Server. Many will use makefiles to make (no pun intended) your life easier.
